## 📄 2. Full Store Description

Windows has a notorious blind spot: **it cannot display battery levels for 2.4GHz wireless USB dongle mice, mechanical keyboards, gaming controllers, or TWS earbuds directly in your taskbar system tray**. Peripheral manufacturers force you to keep bloated, resource-heavy background suites running (Razer Synapse, Logitech G HUB, Corsair iCUE, etc.) just to check if your mouse or keyboard is about to die mid-game or mid-work.

**Battery Indicator** delivers an ultra-lightweight, native, bloat-free Windows 11 utility that monitors battery telemetry directly across all your wireless peripherals and displays them right beside your Windows clock.

### 🌟 Highlights & Core Capabilities:

- **🔘 Dedicated System Tray Battery Gauges (`+` Pin Button):**
  Pin independent, live circular battery gauge icons to your taskbar for any peripheral. Features active color-coded circular progress rings (Emerald Green >30%, Amber Orange 16–30%, Crimson Red ≤15%) with the device's silhouette in the center and a live charging bolt indicator. Pin multiple devices at once!

- **⚡ 2.4GHz USB Wireless Dongle Telemetry:**
  Reverse-engineered, low-level HID communication for 2.4GHz dongles:
  - **Gaming Mice:** Logitech Lightspeed & Unifying (G Pro, Superlight, G502), Razer HyperSpeed (DeathAdder, Viper, Basilisk), ATK / VXE / Compx mice, Corsair Slipstream, SteelSeries Aerox, Glorious Model O/D.
  - **Mechanical Keyboards:** Keychron (K-Pro, Q-Max, V-Max), NuPhy (Air/Halo), Akko, Epomaker, Portronics Hydra 10, and generic Areson wireless keyboards.

- **🎧 True Wireless Stereo (TWS) Dual-Bud Telemetry:**
  Displays independent **Left (`L`)** and **Right (`R`)** battery percentages with explicit connection mode tagging (e.g., `Bluetooth • L: 85% R: 90%`). Supports OnePlus Nord Buds 3r, OnePlus Buds Pro 2, Oppo Enco, Realme, Sony WF-1000XM4/XM5, and Apple AirPods.

- **🎮 Wireless Gamepads & Controllers:**
  Seamless battery monitoring for Xbox Wireless Controllers (Series X|S, One, Elite), PlayStation DualSense (PS5) & DualShock 4 (PS4), Nintendo Switch Pro Controllers, and 8BitDo wireless gamepads.

- **🔌 Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Telemetry:**
  Monitors power levels and runtime status for APC, CyberPower, and standard HID Power Device UPS backup systems.

- **🧩 Crowdsourced Device Profiles (Plugin / JSON Database):**
  Zero-code community expansion! Support new wireless mice, mechanical keyboards, or custom dongles simply by dropping a `.json` profile into your local Profiles folder—no compiling required.

- **⌨️ Customizable Global Summon Shortcut:**
  Instantly summon the battery flyout from anywhere using a global hotkey (**Default: `Win + Alt + B`**). Customize to any keybind combination (Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win + Key) with our interactive setup dialog.

- **✏️ Custom Device Aliases & Hide Capabilities:**
  Right-click any device card to assign a custom nickname or hide unwanted secondary devices. Unhide all devices in one click.

- **⚠️ Smart Low Battery Notifications:**
  Configurable Windows notifications when battery levels drop below a customizable threshold (default: <20%). Built-in cooldown safeguards prevent spam.

- **🛡️ 100% Telemetry-Free Privacy Guarantee:**
  Battery Indicator is completely offline. Zero telemetry, tracking, analytics, or hardware metrics are transmitted to any server. When errors occur, sanitized diagnostic logs (with all personal paths redacted) are kept strictly on your local disk.
